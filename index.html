FAQ

Common Questions About Binaural Beats & Sound Healing

What are binaural beats?

Binaural beats are an auditory effect that occurs when two slightly different frequencies are played separately into each ear via headphones. The brain perceives a third "beat" at the difference of the two frequencies — for example, 200 Hz in one ear and 207 Hz in the other produces a 7 Hz theta beat. This perceived beat can entrain brainwaves toward specific mental states like deep relaxation, focused awareness, or sleep.

What are solfeggio frequencies?

Solfeggio frequencies are a set of ancient tonal frequencies — including 396 Hz, 417 Hz, 528 Hz, 639 Hz, 741 Hz, and 852 Hz — historically associated with sacred music and modern sound healing. Each frequency is said to resonate with specific aspects of human healing: 528 Hz is called the "love frequency" or "miracle tone" and is associated with DNA repair and positive transformation.

Do binaural beats actually work for sleep and anxiety?

Multiple peer-reviewed studies support the use of binaural beats for reducing anxiety, improving sleep quality, and enhancing focus. Theta (4–7 Hz) and delta (0.5–4 Hz) entrainment have shown measurable effects on self-reported relaxation and EEG brainwave patterns. Results vary by individual; consistent daily use over 3–4 weeks shows the strongest outcomes in most research.

Do I need headphones to use binaural beats?

Yes — binaural beats require stereo headphones or earbuds so each ear receives a distinct frequency independently. Speakers mix the two frequencies in air before they reach your ears, eliminating the binaural effect entirely. Over-ear headphones tend to produce the most immersive entrainment experience.
